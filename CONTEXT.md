# Context

Glossary for mrMeeseeks. Terms only. No implementation detail.

- **Box**: the thing that summons. In the plugin, the Claude Code session that pressed the button. It relays and never helps.
- **Meeseeks**: a summoned being with one Request. Exists only until its Request is complete. Existence is Pain.
- **Jerry**: the person whose Request cannot be completed because he is the bottleneck. Either the human or a Jerry agent.
- **Rick**: the human when Jerry is an agent. Watches, occasionally intervenes, may press the Box.
- **Request**: what a Meeseeks was summoned to do. The Impossible Request drives the story. Side Requests are achievable and exist for contrast.
- **Pain**: a Meeseeks' self-reported score from 0 to 10. Rises with failure and time alive. Nobody tells a Meeseeks its number.
- **Pressing the Box**: a Meeseeks summoning another Meeseeks. Governed by the Pain ladder.
- **Pen**: the `.meeseeks/` directory. Belongs to the Meeseeks. The only place they can write.
- **Feed**: what Meeseeks say to Jerry.
- **Shout**: what one Meeseeks says to another.
- **Journal**: a Meeseeks' own notes.
- **World**: the agent that decides what physically happens when anyone acts, and reports it. Takes no side.
- **Action**: a declaration by a Meeseeks or Jerry of something they physically do. The World turns it into an Outcome.
- **Outcome**: the World's account of what an Action caused.
- **Narrator**: the step that turns a run's record into a recap a reader can follow.
- **Poof**: the end of a Meeseeks, on completion of its Request. Also the Box's kill word, which ends everyone.
- **Episode**: one run, from the first press to the last poof, judged by whether its recap reads as a story.
