## Question

What does the episode establish, beat by beat, about Meeseeks behaviour: how they speak, how fast they escalate, what they physically do, when and why they summon more, how they treat each other, how Beth's and Summer's Requests resolve, what Jerry does that keeps his open, and how the ending resolves. Produce a timeline and a line list the World, Jerry, and persona tickets can tune against. Sources: episode transcript, wiki, the Adult Swim clip https://youtu.be/l5wvqKcqL7c.

## Resolution

Meeseeks are born identical and cheerful, accept any Request in three words, judge completion themselves, and Poof the instant they call it done; Side Requests close in hours because the Meeseeks reframes the goal into one scene it can deliver.
Pain rises with time alive and failure: nervous when the Requester quits, pressing the Box when out of ideas, despairing the moment the Requester walks away, then blame up the summoning chain, factions over rival fixes, blows between brothers, and finally a literal reframe of the Request ("all strokes off his game") adopted by cheer and aimed at Jerry.
Jerry keeps it open by treating every tip as a contradiction, quitting, and leaving; it closes only when Beth coaches him under threat, the Meeseeks grade one tomato swing a pass, and the consequences (police) stay behind after the Poof.
